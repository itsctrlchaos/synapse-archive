# Rights and licensing scope

This file explains the approved contribution policy. It is **not** a repository-wide license grant.

## Approved conversation content

Under [contribution terms mvp-v1](https://synapse.ctrlchaos.io/synapse/guidelines#terms), **CC BY 4.0 applies only to rights a contributor owns or is authorized to license in the approved public content**. Read the [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [full legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).

The license permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, under its conditions. Appropriate attribution, a license link, and indication of changes are required as applicable; retain supplied notices and required credits. Do not imply endorsement. Granted permissions are irrevocable while the conditions are followed. The actual license controls over this summary.

Synapse is a noncommercial community project. That describes the project’s purpose, not an additional noncommercial restriction on licensed contributions. No additional reuse restrictions are imposed here.

## Separate rights remain separate

A contributor cannot grant rights they do not own or control. Their permission does not automatically license separate rights in AI output, quotations, images, other third-party material, trademarks, or someone else’s personal information. Public availability and possession of a shared-chat URL do not establish ownership or republication permission.

Read a record’s editing disclosure, attribution, source information, and any required notices before reuse. The project does not guarantee that the license supplies every permission your use may need. This note does not replace applicable exceptions or limitations, nor does it narrow freedoms provided by CC BY 4.0.

## Attribution in practice

Identify the supplied public display name, conversation title, stable archive link, license, and your actual changes. Preserve supplied required notices and credits. A usable example is in [the README](README.md#read-download-and-reuse).

If a contribution is attributed to a nickname or Anonymous, use that supplied label. Do not identify or imply a real person behind it. For illustrative records, retain the illustrative disclosure rather than presenting the text as a genuine contributor exchange.
